Privacy Policy

What information this website uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not send your reservation details to our servers. What you enter stays in your own browser unless you share it with us directly.

Information kept in your browser

  • Reservations: when you book a table, the name, date, time, party size and table you choose are saved in your browser’s local storage so the dining room can show your booking. You can remove them at any time with “Manage bookings” on the Reservation page, or by clearing your browser’s site data.
  • Sound preference: if you mute the menu scroll, that choice is remembered in the same way.

This information never leaves your device through this website, and we cannot see it.

Third-party services

To load fonts and animation libraries, your browser requests files from Google Fonts, cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. Like any website, these services receive your IP address and basic browser information when the files are downloaded. Their own privacy policies explain how they handle it.

Cookies

The site does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

Information you give us directly

If you contact us or book in person or by phone, we use the details you give us only to look after your visit and reply to you, and we keep them only as long as we need to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Speak to our team and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
